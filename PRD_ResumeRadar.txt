1. OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Product Name    : ResumeRadar
GitHub Repo     : resume-radar  (github.com/<org>/resume-radar)
Tagline         : "Upload your resume. Radar finds the rest."
Type            : Web Application
Target Users    : Job seekers (students, fresh graduates, working professionals)
Primary Goal    : Automate job/internship discovery by analyzing a user's resume
                  and surfacing the most relevant opportunities, along with the
                  hiring contact and a ready-to-send outreach email.

One-line pitch  : Upload your resume, pick a city — get the right jobs, the
                  right contact, and the right email to send.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. PROBLEM STATEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Job seekers spend hours:
  - Manually browsing job boards that return irrelevant results
  - Guessing which roles actually match their skill set
  - Hunting for the right person to contact at a company
  - Writing cold emails from scratch with little idea of what works

There is no single tool that connects resume analysis, role matching,
contact discovery, and personalized outreach into one seamless workflow.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. GOALS AND SUCCESS METRICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Goal 1  : Reduce time from "resume ready" to "application sent" to under
          10 minutes.

Goal 2  : Surface only roles where at least 60% of the required skills
          overlap with the user's resume.

Goal 3  : Provide a verified or highly probable hiring contact for each
          matched role.

Goal 4  : Generate a personalized outreach email that references the user's
          actual experience and the specific role.

Success Metrics
  - Time-to-first-application < 10 minutes (measured via session analytics)
  - User satisfaction score >= 4.2 / 5.0
  - Email open rate on generated drafts >= 35% (tracked via opt-in feedback)
  - Match relevance rating >= 4.0 / 5.0 (post-session survey)


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. TARGET USERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Persona A — Engineering Student (Primary)
  Profile   : BTech / BE student, 2nd to 4th year or recent graduate
  Goal      : Find internships or entry-level roles in their field
  Pain Point: Does not know how to position themselves or who to reach out to

Persona B — Working Professional (Secondary)
  Profile   : 2–8 years of experience, looking to switch roles or companies
  Goal      : Find mid-senior roles matching their current skill set
  Pain Point: Too busy to search manually; wants targeted, high-signal leads

Persona C — Career Switcher
  Profile   : Professional pivoting to a new domain (e.g., from mechanical
              engineering to product management or data analytics)
  Goal      : Identify roles where transferable skills are valued
  Pain Point: Unsure which roles are realistic given their background


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. SCOPE — VERSION 1.0
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

IN SCOPE
  - PDF resume upload and AI-based parsing
  - Skill, experience, and education extraction from resume
  - Location selector (city / country / remote)
  - AI-powered job/internship matching against live or cached listings
  - Match score and reasoning for each result
  - Hiring contact suggestion per role (name, title, LinkedIn, email if public)
  - Personalized outreach email generation per role
  - Copy-to-clipboard for email
  - Basic user session (no login required for v1)

OUT OF SCOPE (deferred to v2+)
  - One-click application submission
  - Resume editing / optimization suggestions
  - ATS score / keyword gap analysis
  - Saved searches and job alerts
  - Company culture or salary benchmarking
  - Mobile native apps


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. FEATURE REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

6.1  RESUME UPLOAD AND PARSING
  - Accept PDF files up to 5 MB
  - Extract: full name, contact info, skills (technical and soft), work
    experience (company, role, duration), education, certifications, projects
  - Display a parsed summary to the user for confirmation before proceeding
  - Flag if the resume cannot be parsed and prompt re-upload

6.2  LOCATION SELECTION
  - Allow selection of one or more cities / countries
  - Include a "Remote" option
  - Default to user's detected region (browser geolocation, no storage)

6.3  JOB MATCHING
  - Query job sources (API integrations or scraped data — see Section 8)
  - Score each listing against the user's extracted profile using AI
  - Return top 10 matched roles, ranked by match score (0–100)
  - Each result must show:
      * Job title and company name
      * Location and work mode (remote / hybrid / on-site)
      * Match score and a 2–3 line explanation of why it matched
      * Date posted
      * Link to the original job listing

6.4  HIRING CONTACT DISCOVERY
  - For each matched role, surface a relevant contact:
      * Preferred: recruiter or HR contact at the company
      * Fallback: hiring manager, team lead, or department head
  - Show: name, job title, LinkedIn profile URL, email (if publicly available)
  - Clearly indicate confidence level (Verified / Likely / Estimated)
  - Do not fabricate contacts — show "Contact not found" if unavailable

6.5  OUTREACH EMAIL GENERATION
  - Generate a personalized cold email for each role that includes:
      * A subject line tailored to the role
      * A brief intro referencing the user's strongest relevant experience
      * 1–2 specific skills that match the role requirements
      * A clear, low-pressure call to action
  - Keep emails under 200 words
  - Provide a "Regenerate" option for a different tone or angle
  - One-click copy to clipboard

6.6  RESULTS PAGE — UI REQUIREMENTS
  - Card layout: one card per matched job
  - Each card expands to show contact details and email draft
  - Filter bar: filter by match score, location, job type (internship / full-time)
  - Loading state with progress indicator during analysis


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. USER FLOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Step 1  : User lands on homepage
          → Sees a clear CTA: "Upload your resume to find matching jobs"

Step 2  : User uploads PDF resume
          → System parses the resume using AI
          → Displays extracted profile summary (skills, experience, education)
          → User confirms or edits the summary

Step 3  : User selects preferred location(s) and job type
          (internship / full-time / both)

Step 4  : System runs AI matching
          → Shows a loading screen with status updates
          → Returns top 10 matched roles

Step 5  : User browses results
          → Clicks a job card to expand details
          → Views hiring contact and generated email

Step 6  : User copies email, clicks through to job listing, or regenerates email

Step 7  : Optional — user downloads the full results as a PDF or CSV

8. TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

8.1  FRONTEND (Deployed on Vercel)
  - Framework      : Next.js 14+ (App Router)
  - Hosting        : Vercel (auto-deploy from main branch of resume-radar repo)
  - Styling        : Tailwind CSS
  - File Upload    : Client-side PDF → base64 encoding before API call
  - State          : Session-scoped via React state (no auth in v1)
  - Env Variables  : NEXT_PUBLIC_API_URL pointing to the Render backend

  Vercel Configuration (vercel.json)
  - Root directory : /frontend
  - Build command  : next build
  - Output dir     : .next
  - Environment    : NEXT_PUBLIC_API_URL, ANTHROPIC_API_KEY (if calling AI
                     directly from Next.js API routes for lightweight ops)

8.2  BACKEND (Deployed on Render)
  - Framework      : FastAPI (Python) or Express.js (Node)
  - Hosting        : Render Web Service (auto-deploy from resume-radar repo)
  - Root directory : /backend
  - Build command  : pip install -r requirements.txt  (or npm install)
  - Start command  : uvicorn main:app --host 0.0.0.0 --port 8000
                     (or node index.js for Node)
  - Plan           : Render Starter ($7/month) or free tier for prototyping
                     Note: Render free tier spins down after inactivity;
                     upgrade to Starter for production use

  Render Configuration (render.yaml — committed to repo root)
  - Service type   : Web Service
  - Region         : Oregon (us-west) or Singapore (ap-southeast) based on
                     primary user base
  - Health check   : GET /health → 200 OK

  Backend Responsibilities
  - PDF parsing via Claude API
  - Job matching logic and scoring
  - Contact discovery API orchestration
  - Email generation via Claude API
  - Rate limiting and abuse prevention

8.4  AI / BACKEND SERVICES
  - Resume Parsing   : Claude API (claude-sonnet-4-6) via /v1/messages
  - Job Matching     : Claude API with structured job listing data as context
  - Email Generation : Claude API with resume summary + job description
  - Web Search       : Anthropic web_search tool for live job discovery

8.5  JOB DATA SOURCES (priority order)
  1. LinkedIn Jobs API (or approved scraping methods)
  2. Indeed API / Glassdoor API
  3. Naukri API (India-specific)
  4. Internshala API (internships, India)
  5. Remotive / WeWorkRemotely (remote roles)

8.6  CONTACT DISCOVERY
  - LinkedIn People Search (via API or approved integration)
  - Hunter.io or similar email finder API (with confidence scoring)
  - Fallback: publicly listed company page contacts

8.7  DATA PRIVACY
  - Resume content is not stored after the session ends
  - No PII is sent to third parties beyond what is needed for matching
  - User must acknowledge a privacy notice before upload
  - Backend does not write resume files to disk; processes in memory only

8.8  PERFORMANCE TARGETS
  - Resume parsing        < 5 seconds
  - Job matching + contact discovery < 20 seconds total
  - Email generation per role < 3 seconds
  - Vercel cold start     < 1 second (Edge runtime for lightweight routes)
  - Render cold start     < 3 seconds (on paid plan; ~30s on free tier)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
12. RISKS AND MITIGATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Risk 1  : Low-quality resume parsing for poorly formatted PDFs
          Mitigation: Detect low-text PDFs and prompt user to re-upload or
          use a text-based version

Risk 2  : Job listing APIs may have rate limits or go down
          Mitigation: Cache recent listings; maintain fallback to at least
          two job sources

Risk 3  : Contact details may be outdated or inaccurate
          Mitigation: Show confidence level prominently; disclaim that users
          should verify before reaching out

Risk 4  : Generated emails may feel generic despite personalization
          Mitigation: Allow users to provide additional context to improve output

Risk 5  : Misuse — bulk email generation or spam
          Mitigation: Rate limiting, session caps, and usage monitoring

Risk 6  : Render free tier spin-down causing poor first-load experience
          Mitigation: Upgrade to Render Starter ($7/month) before beta launch;
          add a frontend loading indicator that accounts for cold start latency
